At this point our Basket Catch game should have all of its major components finished, you should be able to play your game at this point. However, this does not mean there isn't more that would could do to improve how our game plays. We would refer to this stage of development as the "polish" phase. In this stage we are fixing small bugs or inconsistencies with our game. 

## Let's polish this game up!

In your Basket Catch project do the following:

1. Make sure that all of your scenes have a background (Your start screen, main scene, and end screen)
2. Find a font online and replace your label's default font with your new font.
3. Make sure none of your images are the default icon.svg image.
4. Edit where your fruit can be dropped to more accurately fit on your play area.
5. Make it so your player's y position cannot change (stop it from being pushed downward.)
6. Make your drop and catch sound effects play back at a random pitch to give it more variety. (use the pitch_scale property for this)
7. For extra credit:
	1. Play a sound effect when you press the space bar
	2. Have your end screen display your highest achieved score as well
